  <section id="pretrained-models">
<h1>Pretrained Models<a class="headerlink" href="#pretrained-models" title="Link to this heading"></a></h1>
<p>We provide various pre-trained Sentence Transformers models via our Sentence Transformers Hugging Face organization. Additionally, over 6,000 community Sentence Transformers models have been publicly released on the Hugging Face Hub. All models can be found here:</p>
<ul class="simple">
<li><p><strong>Original models</strong>: <a class="reference external" href="https://huggingface.co/models?library=sentence-transformers&amp;author=sentence-transformers">Sentence Transformers Hugging Face organization</a>.</p></li>
<li><p><strong>Community models</strong>: <a class="reference external" href="https://huggingface.co/models?library=sentence-transformers">All Sentence Transformer models on Hugging Face</a>.</p></li>
</ul>
<p>Each of these models can be easily downloaded and used like so:</p>
<aside class="sidebar">
<p class="sidebar-title">Original Models</p>
<p>For the original models from the <a class="reference external" href="https://huggingface.co/models?library=sentence-transformers&amp;author=sentence-transformers">Sentence Transformers Hugging Face organization</a>, it is not necessary to include the model author or organization prefix. For example, this snippet loads <a class="reference external" href="https://huggingface.co/sentence-transformers/all-mpnet-base-v2">sentence-transformers/all-mpnet-base-v2</a>.</p>
</aside>
<div class="highlight-python notranslate"><div class="highlight"><pre><span></span><span class="kn">from</span> <span class="nn">sentence_transformers</span> <span class="kn">import</span> <span class="n">SentenceTransformer</span>

<span class="c1"># Load https://huggingface.co/sentence-transformers/all-mpnet-base-v2</span>
<span class="n">model</span> <span class="o">=</span> <span class="n">SentenceTransformer</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="s2">&quot;sentence-transformers/all-mpnet-base-v2&quot;</span><span class="p">)</span>
<span class="n">embeddings</span> <span class="o">=</span> <span class="n">model</span><span class="o">.</span><span class="n">encode</span><span class="p">([</span>
    <span class="s2">&quot;The weather is lovely today.&quot;</span><span class="p">,</span>
    <span class="s2">&quot;It&#39;s so sunny outside!&quot;</span><span class="p">,</span>
    <span class="s2">&quot;He drove to the stadium.&quot;</span><span class="p">,</span>
<span class="p">])</span>
<span class="n">similarities</span> <span class="o">=</span> <span class="n">model</span><span class="o">.</span><span class="n">similarity</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="n">embeddings</span><span class="p">,</span> <span class="n">embeddings</span><span class="p">)</span>
</pre></div>
</div>
<div class="admonition note">
<p class="admonition-title">Note</p>
<p>Consider using the <a class="reference external" href="https://huggingface.co/spaces/mteb/leaderboard">Massive Textual Embedding Benchmark leaderboard</a> as an inspiration of strong Sentence Transformer models. Be wary:</p>
<ul class="simple">
<li><p><strong>Model sizes</strong>: it is recommended to filter away the large models that might not be feasible without excessive hardware.</p></li>
<li><p><strong>Experimentation is key</strong>: models that perform well on the leaderboard do not necessarily do well on your tasks, it is <strong>crucial</strong> to experiment with various promising models.</p></li>
</ul>
</div>
<div class="admonition tip">
<p class="admonition-title">Tip</p>
<p>Read <a class="reference external" href="./usage/efficiency.html">Sentence Transformer &gt; Usage &gt; Speeding up Inference</a> for tips on how to speed up inference of models by up to 2x-3x.</p>
</div>

<section id="multilingual-models">
<h2>Multilingual Models<a class="headerlink" href="#multilingual-models" title="Link to this heading"></a></h2>
<p>The following models similar embeddings for the same texts in different languages. You do not need to specify the input language. Details are in our publication <a class="reference external" href="https://huggingface.co/papers/2004.09813">Making Monolingual Sentence Embeddings Multilingual using Knowledge Distillation</a>. We used the following 50+ languages: ar, bg, ca, cs, da, de, el, en, es, et, fa, fi, fr, fr-ca, gl, gu, he, hi, hr, hu, hy, id, it, ja, ka, ko, ku, lt, lv, mk, mn, mr, ms, my, nb, nl, pl, pt, pt-br, ro, ru, sk, sl, sq, sr, sv, th, tr, uk, ur, vi, zh-cn, zh-tw.</p>
<section id="semantic-similarity-models">
<h3>Semantic Similarity Models<a class="headerlink" href="#semantic-similarity-models" title="Link to this heading"></a></h3>
<p>These models find semantically similar sentences within one language or across languages:</p>
<ul class="simple">
<li><p><strong><a class="reference external" href="https://huggingface.co/sentence-transformers/distiluse-base-multilingual-cased-v1">sentence-transformers/distiluse-base-multilingual-cased-v1</a></strong>: Multilingual knowledge distilled version of <a class="reference external" href="https://huggingface.co/papers/1907.04307">multilingual Universal Sentence Encoder</a>. Supports 15 languages:  Arabic, Chinese, Dutch, English, French, German, Italian, Korean, Polish, Portuguese, Russian, Spanish, Turkish.</p></li>
<li><p><strong><a class="reference external" href="https://huggingface.co/sentence-transformers/distiluse-base-multilingual-cased-v2">sentence-transformers/distiluse-base-multilingual-cased-v2</a></strong>: Multilingual knowledge distilled version of <a class="reference external" href="https://huggingface.co/papers/1907.04307">multilingual Universal Sentence Encoder</a>. This version supports 50+ languages, but performs a bit weaker than the v1 model.</p></li>
<li><p><strong><a class="reference external" href="https://huggingface.co/sentence-transformers/paraphrase-multilingual-MiniLM-L12-v2">sentence-transformers/paraphrase-multilingual-MiniLM-L12-v2</a></strong> - Multilingual version of <a class="reference external" href="https://huggingface.co/sentence-transformers/paraphrase-MiniLM-L12-v2">sentence-transformers/paraphrase-MiniLM-L12-v2</a>, trained on parallel data for 50+ languages.</p></li>
<li><p><strong><a class="reference external" href="https://huggingface.co/sentence-transformers/paraphrase-multilingual-mpnet-base-v2">sentence-transformers/paraphrase-multilingual-mpnet-base-v2</a></strong> - Multilingual version of <a class="reference external" href="https://huggingface.co/sentence-transformers/paraphrase-mpnet-base-v2">sentence-transformers/paraphrase-mpnet-base-v2</a>, trained on parallel data for 50+ languages.</p></li>
</ul>
</section>
<section id="bitext-mining">
<h3>Bitext Mining<a class="headerlink" href="#bitext-mining" title="Link to this heading"></a></h3>
<p>Bitext mining describes the process of finding translated sentence pairs in two languages. If this is your use-case, the following model gives the best performance:</p>
<ul class="simple">
<li><p><strong><a class="reference external" href="https://huggingface.co/sentence-transformers/LaBSE">sentence-transformers/LaBSE</a></strong> - <a class="reference external" href="https://huggingface.co/papers/2007.01852">LaBSE</a> Model. Supports 109 languages. Works well for finding translation pairs in multiple languages. As detailed  <a class="reference external" href="https://huggingface.co/papers/2004.09813">here</a>, LaBSE works less well for assessing the similarity of sentence pairs that are not translations of each other.</p></li>
</ul>
<p>Extending a model to new languages is easy by following <a class="reference internal" href="../../examples/sentence_transformer/training/multilingual/README.html"><span class="std std-doc">Training Examples &gt; Multilingual Models</span></a>.</p>
</section>
</section>
<section id="multimodal-models">
<h2>Multimodal Models<a class="headerlink" href="#multimodal-models" title="Link to this heading"></a></h2>
<p>Sentence Transformers supports multimodal models that can embed text alongside images, audio, or video into a joint vector space. This enables cross-modal tasks like text-to-image search, image-to-image search, image clustering, and zero-shot classification.</p>
<div class="admonition seealso">
<p class="admonition-title">See also</p>
<p>See the <a class="reference external" href="https://huggingface.co/blog/multimodal-sentence-transformers">Multimodal Embedding &amp; Reranker Models</a> blogpost for an inference walkthrough and an overview of the multimodal models supported at the time of writing.</p>
</div>
<div class="admonition tip">
<p class="admonition-title">Tip</p>
<p>Multimodal models require additional dependencies. Install them with e.g. <code class="docutils literal notranslate"><span class="pre">pip</span> <span class="pre">install</span> <span class="pre">-U</span> <span class="pre">&quot;sentence-transformers[image]&quot;</span></code> for image support. See <a class="reference external" href="../installation.html">Installation</a> for all options.</p>
</div>
<p>You can check which modalities a model supports using the <a class="reference internal" href="../package_reference/sentence_transformer/model.html#sentence_transformers.sentence_transformer.model.SentenceTransformer.modalities" title="sentence_transformers.sentence_transformer.model.SentenceTransformer.modalities"><code class="xref py py-attr docutils literal notranslate"><span class="pre">modalities</span></code></a> property and <a class="reference internal" href="../package_reference/sentence_transformer/model.html#sentence_transformers.sentence_transformer.model.SentenceTransformer.supports" title="sentence_transformers.sentence_transformer.model.SentenceTransformer.supports"><code class="xref py py-meth docutils literal notranslate"><span class="pre">supports()</span></code></a> method:</p>
<div class="highlight-python notranslate"><div class="highlight"><pre><span></span><span class="kn">from</span> <span class="nn">sentence_transformers</span> <span class="kn">import</span> <span class="n">SentenceTransformer</span>

<span class="n">model</span> <span class="o">=</span> <span class="n">SentenceTransformer</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="s2">&quot;Qwen/Qwen3-VL-Embedding-2B&quot;</span><span class="p">)</span>
<span class="nb">print</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="n">model</span><span class="o">.</span><span class="n">modalities</span><span class="p">)</span>
<span class="c1"># =&gt; [&#39;text&#39;, &#39;image&#39;, &#39;video&#39;, &#39;message&#39;]</span>
<span class="nb">print</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="n">model</span><span class="o">.</span><span class="n">supports</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="s2">&quot;image&quot;</span><span class="p">))</span>
<span class="c1"># =&gt; True</span>
<span class="nb">print</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="n">model</span><span class="o">.</span><span class="n">supports</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="s2">&quot;audio&quot;</span><span class="p">))</span>
<span class="c1"># =&gt; False</span>
</pre></div>
</div>
<section id="image-text-models">
<h3>Image &amp; Text Models<a class="headerlink" href="#image-text-models" title="Link to this heading"></a></h3>
<p>Image-text models embed both images and text into the same vector space.</p>
<p>The original Sentence Transformers image-text models are based on CLIP. See <a class="reference internal" href="../../examples/sentence_transformer/applications/image-search/README.html"><span class="std std-doc">Usage &gt; Image Search</span></a> for details on text-to-image search, image-to-image search, image clustering, and zero-shot image classification.</p>
<p>The following CLIP models are available with their respective Top 1 accuracy on zero-shot ImageNet validation dataset:</p>
<table border="1" class="docutils">
<thead>
<tr>
<th>Model</th>
<th style="text-align: center;">Top 1 Performance</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr>
<td><a href="https://huggingface.co/sentence-transformers/clip-ViT-L-14">sentence-transformers/clip-ViT-L-14</a></td>
<td style="text-align: center;">75.4</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><a href="https://huggingface.co/sentence-transformers/clip-ViT-B-16">sentence-transformers/clip-ViT-B-16</a></td>
<td style="text-align: center;">68.1</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><a href="https://huggingface.co/sentence-transformers/clip-ViT-B-32">sentence-transformers/clip-ViT-B-32</a></td>
<td style="text-align: center;">63.3</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
<p>We further provide this multilingual text-image model:</p>
<ul class="simple">
<li><p><strong><a class="reference external" href="https://huggingface.co/sentence-transformers/clip-ViT-B-32-multilingual-v1">sentence-transformers/clip-ViT-B-32-multilingual-v1</a></strong> - Multilingual text encoder for the <a class="reference external" href="https://huggingface.co/sentence-transformers/clip-ViT-B-32">sentence-transformers/clip-ViT-B-32</a> model using <a class="reference external" href="https://huggingface.co/papers/2004.09813">Multilingual Knowledge Distillation</a>. This model can encode text in 50+ languages to match the image vectors from the <a class="reference external" href="https://huggingface.co/sentence-transformers/clip-ViT-B-32">sentence-transformers/clip-ViT-B-32</a> model.</p></li>
</ul>
<p>Newer multimodal models use the unified <a class="reference internal" href="../package_reference/base/modules.html#sentence_transformers.base.modules.Transformer" title="sentence_transformers.base.modules.Transformer"><code class="xref py py-class docutils literal notranslate"><span class="pre">Transformer</span></code></a> module, which automatically detects supported modalities from the underlying model and processor. These models typically support richer input formats, including interleaved image-text inputs via chat messages. Notable examples include:</p>
<ul class="simple">
<li><p><strong><a class="reference external" href="https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-VL-Embedding-2B">Qwen/Qwen3-VL-Embedding-2B</a></strong> - A Qwen3-VL-based embedding model that supports text, image, and interleaved image-text inputs via message format. Useful for document screenshot embedding and cross-modal retrieval. See <a class="reference internal" href="../../examples/sentence_transformer/training/multimodal/README.html"><span class="std std-doc">Training Examples &gt; Multimodal</span></a> for a finetuning example.</p></li>
</ul>
<p>VLM-based models support additional modalities and input formats compared to CLIP models. You can verify this with <a class="reference internal" href="../package_reference/sentence_transformer/model.html#sentence_transformers.sentence_transformer.model.SentenceTransformer.modalities" title="sentence_transformers.sentence_transformer.model.SentenceTransformer.modalities"><code class="xref py py-attr docutils literal notranslate"><span class="pre">modalities</span></code></a>:</p>
<div class="highlight-python notranslate"><div class="highlight"><pre><span></span><span class="kn">from</span> <span class="nn">sentence_transformers</span> <span class="kn">import</span> <span class="n">SentenceTransformer</span>

<span class="n">model</span> <span class="o">=</span> <span class="n">SentenceTransformer</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="s2">&quot;Qwen/Qwen3-VL-Embedding-2B&quot;</span><span class="p">)</span>
<span class="nb">print</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="n">model</span><span class="o">.</span><span class="n">modalities</span><span class="p">)</span>
<span class="c1"># =&gt; [&#39;text&#39;, &#39;image&#39;, &#39;video&#39;, &#39;message&#39;]</span>
</pre></div>
</div>
<p>The <code class="docutils literal notranslate"><span class="pre">&quot;message&quot;</span></code> modality means the model accepts chat-style message inputs, allowing you to combine text and images in a single input. This is how VLM-based models handle interleaved multimodal content.</p>
</section>
<section id="audio-video-models">
<h3>Audio &amp; Video Models<a class="headerlink" href="#audio-video-models" title="Link to this heading"></a></h3>
<p>The <a class="reference internal" href="../package_reference/base/modules.html#sentence_transformers.base.modules.Transformer" title="sentence_transformers.base.modules.Transformer"><code class="xref py py-class docutils literal notranslate"><span class="pre">Transformer</span></code></a> module also supports audio and video modalities when the underlying model and processor provide them. Audio models accept file paths, numpy/torch arrays, dicts with <code class="docutils literal notranslate"><span class="pre">&quot;array&quot;</span></code> and <code class="docutils literal notranslate"><span class="pre">&quot;sampling_rate&quot;</span></code> keys, or <code class="docutils literal notranslate"><span class="pre">torchcodec.AudioDecoder</span></code> instances, while video models accept file paths, numpy/torch arrays, dicts with <code class="docutils literal notranslate"><span class="pre">&quot;array&quot;</span></code> and <code class="docutils literal notranslate"><span class="pre">&quot;video_metadata&quot;</span></code> keys, or <code class="docutils literal notranslate"><span class="pre">torchcodec.VideoDecoder</span></code> instances. As pretrained audio and video embedding models become available on the Hugging Face Hub, they can be loaded with <a class="reference internal" href="../package_reference/sentence_transformer/model.html#sentence_transformers.sentence_transformer.model.SentenceTransformer" title="sentence_transformers.sentence_transformer.model.SentenceTransformer"><code class="xref py py-class docutils literal notranslate"><span class="pre">SentenceTransformer</span></code></a> just like any other model.</p>
</section>
</section>
<section id="instructor-models">
<h2>INSTRUCTOR models<a class="headerlink" href="#instructor-models" title="Link to this heading"></a></h2>
<p>Some INSTRUCTOR models, such as <a class="reference external" href="https://huggingface.co/hkunlp/instructor-large">hkunlp/instructor-large</a>, are natively supported in Sentence Transformers. These models are special, as they are trained with instructions in mind. Notably, the primary difference between normal Sentence Transformer models and Instructor models is that the latter do not include the instructions themselves in the pooling step.</p>
<p>The following models work out of the box:</p>
<ul class="simple">
<li><p><a class="reference external" href="https://huggingface.co/hkunlp/instructor-base">hkunlp/instructor-base</a></p></li>
<li><p><a class="reference external" href="https://huggingface.co/hkunlp/instructor-large">hkunlp/instructor-large</a></p></li>
<li><p><a class="reference external" href="https://huggingface.co/hkunlp/instructor-xl">hkunlp/instructor-xl</a></p></li>
</ul>
<p>You can use these models like so:</p>
<div class="highlight-python notranslate"><div class="highlight"><pre><span></span><span class="kn">from</span> <span class="nn">sentence_transformers</span> <span class="kn">import</span> <span class="n">SentenceTransformer</span>

<span class="n">model</span> <span class="o">=</span> <span class="n">SentenceTransformer</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="s2">&quot;hkunlp/instructor-large&quot;</span><span class="p">)</span>
<span class="n">embeddings</span> <span class="o">=</span> <span class="n">model</span><span class="o">.</span><span class="n">encode</span><span class="p">(</span>
    <span class="p">[</span>
        <span class="s2">&quot;Dynamical Scalar Degree of Freedom in Horava-Lifshitz Gravity&quot;</span><span class="p">,</span>
        <span class="s2">&quot;Comparison of Atmospheric Neutrino Flux Calculations at Low Energies&quot;</span><span class="p">,</span>
        <span class="s2">&quot;Fermion Bags in the Massive Gross-Neveu Model&quot;</span><span class="p">,</span>
        <span class="s2">&quot;QCD corrections to Associated t-tbar-H production at the Tevatron&quot;</span><span class="p">,</span>
    <span class="p">],</span>
    <span class="n">prompt</span><span class="o">=</span><span class="s2">&quot;Represent the Medicine sentence for clustering: &quot;</span><span class="p">,</span>
<span class="p">)</span>
<span class="nb">print</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="n">embeddings</span><span class="o">.</span><span class="n">shape</span><span class="p">)</span>
<span class="c1"># =&gt; (4, 768)</span>
</pre></div>
</div>
<p>For example, for information retrieval:</p>
<div class="highlight-python notranslate"><div class="highlight"><pre><span></span><span class="kn">from</span> <span class="nn">sentence_transformers</span> <span class="kn">import</span> <span class="n">SentenceTransformer</span>
<span class="kn">from</span> <span class="nn">sentence_transformers.util</span> <span class="kn">import</span> <span class="n">cos_sim</span>

<span class="n">model</span> <span class="o">=</span> <span class="n">SentenceTransformer</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="s2">&quot;hkunlp/instructor-large&quot;</span><span class="p">)</span>
<span class="n">query</span> <span class="o">=</span> <span class="s2">&quot;where is the food stored in a yam plant&quot;</span>
<span class="n">query_instruction</span> <span class="o">=</span> <span class="p">(</span>
    <span class="s2">&quot;Represent the Wikipedia question for retrieving supporting documents: &quot;</span>
<span class="p">)</span>
<span class="n">corpus</span> <span class="o">=</span> <span class="p">[</span>
    <span class="s1">&#39;Yams are perennial herbaceous vines native to Africa, Asia, and the Americas and cultivated for the consumption of their starchy tubers in many temperate and tropical regions. The tubers themselves, also called &quot;yams&quot;, come in a variety of forms owing to numerous cultivars and related species.&#39;</span><span class="p">,</span>
    <span class="s2">&quot;The disparate impact theory is especially controversial under the Fair Housing Act because the Act regulates many activities relating to housing, insurance, and mortgage loansâ€”and some scholars have argued that the theory&#39;s use under the Fair Housing Act, combined with extensions of the Community Reinvestment Act, contributed to rise of sub-prime lending and the crash of the U.S. housing market and ensuing global economic recession&quot;</span><span class="p">,</span>
    <span class="s2">&quot;Disparate impact in United States labor law refers to practices in employment, housing, and other areas that adversely affect one group of people of a protected characteristic more than another, even though rules applied by employers or landlords are formally neutral. Although the protected classes vary by statute, most federal civil rights laws protect based on race, color, religion, national origin, and sex as protected traits, and some laws include disability status and other traits as well.&quot;</span><span class="p">,</span>
<span class="p">]</span>
<span class="n">corpus_instruction</span> <span class="o">=</span> <span class="s2">&quot;Represent the Wikipedia document for retrieval: &quot;</span>

<span class="n">query_embedding</span> <span class="o">=</span> <span class="n">model</span><span class="o">.</span><span class="n">encode</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="n">query</span><span class="p">,</span> <span class="n">prompt</span><span class="o">=</span><span class="n">query_instruction</span><span class="p">)</span>
<span class="n">corpus_embeddings</span> <span class="o">=</span> <span class="n">model</span><span class="o">.</span><span class="n">encode</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="n">corpus</span><span class="p">,</span> <span class="n">prompt</span><span class="o">=</span><span class="n">corpus_instruction</span><span class="p">)</span>
<span class="n">similarities</span> <span class="o">=</span> <span class="n">cos_sim</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="n">query_embedding</span><span class="p">,</span> <span class="n">corpus_embeddings</span><span class="p">)</span>
<span class="nb">print</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="n">similarities</span><span class="p">)</span>
<span class="c1"># =&gt; tensor([[0.8835, 0.7037, 0.6970]])</span>
</pre></div>
</div>
<p>All other Instructor models either 1) will not load as they refer to <code class="docutils literal notranslate"><span class="pre">InstructorEmbedding</span></code> in their <code class="docutils literal notranslate"><span class="pre">modules.json</span></code> or 2) require calling <code class="docutils literal notranslate"><span class="pre">model.set_pooling_include_prompt(include_prompt=False)</span></code> after loading.</p>
