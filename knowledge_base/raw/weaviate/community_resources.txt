            <div class='post' itemprop="text">
              <p>Hi everyone ! <img src="https://emoji.discourse-cdn.com/twitter/waving_hand.png?v=15" title=":waving_hand:" class="emoji" alt=":waving_hand:" loading="lazy" width="20" height="20"> We have a small (but exciting!) community update to share!</p>
<p>We’ll be <strong>saying goodbye to our public Slack</strong> and officially <strong>moving the conversation here</strong>.</p>
<p>Before you panic — this is actually a glow-up <img src="https://emoji.discourse-cdn.com/twitter/glowing_star.png?v=15" title=":glowing_star:" class="emoji" alt=":glowing_star:" loading="lazy" width="20" height="20"></p>
<p>While Slack has been a great place to connect, there’s one big downside: <em>conversations slowly disappear and aren’t searchable in the long term</em>.</p>
<p>That means valuable answers, insights, and “aha!” <img src="https://emoji.discourse-cdn.com/twitter/light_bulb.png?v=15" title=":light_bulb:" class="emoji" alt=":light_bulb:" loading="lazy" width="20" height="20"> ! moments get lost over time. And we think the knowledge we create as a community deserves better.</p>
<p><a href="https://forum.weaviate.io/">https://forum.weaviate.io/</a></p>
<p>Our forum is built to be a lasting, searchable home for all those great discussions.</p>
<ul>
<li>Better indexing.</li>
<li>Better discoverability.</li>
<li>Better for future-you (and everyone else who runs into the same question six months from now).</li>
</ul>
<p>Think of it as moving from a busy group chat to a cozy, well-organized library… that still feels like a living room. <img src="https://emoji.discourse-cdn.com/twitter/couch_and_lamp.png?v=15" title=":couch_and_lamp:" class="emoji" alt=":couch_and_lamp:" loading="lazy" width="20" height="20"></p>
<p>We’d absolutely love for you to keep the conversation going there — start threads, share ideas, ask questions, and help others.<br>
The community isn’t changing — and neither is the forum — we’re just making it our main address.</p>
<p>And if you ever have a question you’d rather not ask in public, no worries at all.<br>
You can always reach us directly at <a href="mailto:support@weaviate.io">support@weaviate.io</a>. We’re here for you.</p>
<p><strong>Thanks for being part of this community!</strong></p>
<p>We’re really excited about what’s ahead — and we can’t wait to build it together with you!</p>
<p>See you around <img src="https://emoji.discourse-cdn.com/twitter/green_heart.png?v=15" title=":green_heart:" class="emoji" alt=":green_heart:" loading="lazy" width="20" height="20"><br>
The Weaviate Team</p>
